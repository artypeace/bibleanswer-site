# Native Moments player previews

Six full screenshots of the installed Bible Answer app, captured on 2026-10-04
on the existing iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator (iOS 26.3), route:
`-debugTab 3 -debugSection meditations -debugOpenMoment cant_cope`.
No application build or source change was needed for these captures.

The full screens are proportionally downscaled to 720px wide and encoded as WebP.
The surrounding iPhone housing is the same original vector illustration used
elsewhere on this site. The native UI, Scripture and credits are not rewritten.
Sources and checksums are recorded in sources.json; raw captures remain outside
this public repository in the social workspace.

Moments contains music by Marble Space and Arty, as confirmed in Moment.all in
the app's MomentsView.swift. The specific cant_cope player credits Marble Space
for music and Arty for voice. The website's general music credit describes the
collection, not a collaboration credit for this particular recording.
