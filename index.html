I’m anxious and I can’t settle.

Philippians 4:6–7

Be anxious for nothing, but in everything, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.
Berean Standard Bible

For You

Whatever is making you anxious today — bring it to God in prayer. His peace, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your heart.

A Prayer

Lord, I bring my anxious thoughts to You right now, trusting that You will replace them with Your perfect peace. Amen.

Context

Paul wrote this from prison, teaching that peace comes not from circumstances but from entrusting everything to God.

Reading plans

A day is done when the chapter is read, not when the evening comes. You can disappear for a fortnight and come back to the same chapter, waiting.

  • The Gospel of Mark16 daysA chapter a day. The shortest and fastest account of the life of Christ — the easiest place to begin.
  • The Gospel of John21 daysA chapter a day. Who Jesus is and why it changes everything — deeper and slower than the rest.
  • The Proverbs of Solomon31 daysA chapter a day. Short rules about money, words, friends and work — one thought a morning, and nothing to choose.
  • A month of psalms30 daysA psalm a day. Praise, fear, guilt, gratitude, exhaustion — the whole range of what a person brings to God.
  • The New Testament98 daysAbout three chapters a day, in order — Matthew to Revelation.
