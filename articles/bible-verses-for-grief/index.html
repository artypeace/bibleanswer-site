Grief & loss

Bible passages for grief and loss

Read John 11:32–36 and Romans 12:15 in context and find a gentle way to be present with grief after the death of someone you love.

Bible Answer · November 7, 2026 · 2 min read

In this guide

Jesus weeps with the people at the tombWeep with those who weepOne act of presence

Jesus weeps with the people at the tomb

Mary comes to Jesus after Lazarus has died. Jesus sees her and the people with her weeping. John tells us he is moved and that he weeps. Before anything changes at the tomb, grief already has a place in the story.

Weep with those who weep

In Romans 12, Paul describes what love looks like in ordinary relationships. Sharing sorrow can mean listening to the same memory again or sitting together without explaining the loss.

One act of presence

If you are grieving, you can tell someone what would help today: a meal, a walk, or a few minutes of silence together. If you are supporting someone, offer something specific and give them room to decline. A short prayer is enough if that is all you have.
