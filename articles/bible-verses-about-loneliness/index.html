Loneliness & belonging

What does the Bible say about loneliness?

Read John 14:16–18 and Hebrews 10:24–25 in context, with one small way to ask for company when you feel alone.

Bible Answer · November 7, 2026 · 2 min read

In this guide

What Jesus says before leavingWhat we can do for one anotherA small step today

What Jesus says before leaving

Jesus is preparing his disciples for his departure in John 14. He promises the Helper and tells them they will not be left as orphans. That promise belongs to a farewell: the disciples have a reason to be sad, and Jesus still speaks of presence.

What we can do for one another

Hebrews asks readers to think about how they can encourage one another. The point is mutual care. If going to a gathering feels too hard today, start with a conversation with someone you trust.

A small step today

Name what you miss: a voice, a familiar place, or someone who knows how your day went. Then try a specific invitation: “Could we talk for ten minutes this week?” If someone cannot make it, that does not tell you what you are worth.
