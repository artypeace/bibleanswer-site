Evening prayer

How to pray when you have no words

Prayer can begin with a moment of silence or one honest sentence. Romans 8:26–27 describes the Spirit helping when we do not know how to pray.

Bible Answer · November 8, 2026 · 2 min read

Why Paul talks about weakness

In the verses before this passage, Paul writes about waiting and hoping for what we cannot yet see. He includes himself when he says we do not know how to pray. The Spirit’s help meets people in that weakness. A pause in prayer can make room for what you cannot yet say.

A prayer for a quiet moment

Take a moment to notice what you are carrying. If you can name it, tell God in ordinary words. If you can only manage one sentence, try this prayer.

God, I do not know what to say. Help me in my weakness and guide me through the next step.
