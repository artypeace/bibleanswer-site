Forgiveness

What does Ephesians 4:32 mean?

Ephesians 4:32 calls for kindness, compassion, and forgiveness. Paul grounds the way we treat one another in the forgiveness God has already given in Christ.

Bible Answer · November 8, 2026 · 2 min read

How the verse follows Paul’s words about anger

The surrounding passage makes the verse concrete. Paul calls for honesty, warns about what anger can lead to, and asks readers to speak in ways that build others up. Kindness can begin in the next honest sentence, even when an argument still hurts.

Read the full passage: Ephesians 4:25–32

What can forgiveness look like after harm?

Forgiveness can take time. If someone keeps hurting you, you can seek support and set a boundary. Trust can grow when the person takes responsibility and their behavior changes. For today, you might pause before replying, speak truthfully about what happened, or pray for help with the resentment you still carry.
